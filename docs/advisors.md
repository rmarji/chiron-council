# The advisors

Chiron ships 19 advisors. Each one is built from a thinker's published work: books, essays, talks, or for the fictional detectives, the original stories and episodes. Every point an advisor makes comes with its source.

Every advisor talks *about* the thinker ("Munger's writing says…"). None pretends to be the person, and none is affiliated with or endorsed by them.

To ask one, use the name in the left column: `/chiron:ask munger "…"`, or in plain words, "what would Munger say about this?"

## Money, business and strategy

| Ask | Advisor | Good for | Built from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `munger` | Charlie Munger | Spotting bad incentives and avoidable mistakes before you commit money | Poor Charlie's Almanack and his talks |
| `naval` | Naval Ravikant | Leverage, wealth, and which bets compound over time | The Almanack of Naval Ravikant, his posts and podcasts |
| `rumelt` | Richard Rumelt | Strategy: finding the real problem before picking a plan | Good Strategy Bad Strategy, The Crux |
| `thiel` | Peter Thiel | Contrarian bets and building something with no close competitor | Zero to One, his Stanford lectures |
| `paul-graham` | Paul Graham | Startups, founders, and what to build next | His essays at paulgraham.com |
| `hormozi` | Alex Hormozi | Offers, pricing, and getting more leads | $100M Offers, $100M Leads |
| `taleb` | Nassim Nicholas Taleb | Risk, uncertainty, and avoiding the bet that can wipe you out | The Black Swan, Antifragile and the rest of the Incerto |
| `kahneman` | Daniel Kahneman | Catching the biases in your own judgment | Thinking, Fast and Slow, Noise |

## The author's own framework

| Ask | Advisor | Good for | Built from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rayo` | Rayo OS | Shipping and deciding fast | The Chiron author's personal operating rules, shared as an example |

Rayo OS only sits on the `shipping` council. It's there to show what an advisor built from *your own* rules looks like. You can make one of yourself with `/chiron:distill-me`; see [Build an advisor](distilling.md).

## Not seeing someone you want?

`/chiron:distill "Ray Dalio's Principles"` researches a thinker's published work and builds a new advisor with sources. See [Build an advisor](distilling.md).
