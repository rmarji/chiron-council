# Sources — Tim Ferriss

Complete bibliography with dates. Primary sources (Ferriss's own books, talks, essays, and podcast) first; secondary and background sources after. This is where the `provenance.sources` block is drawn from.

## Primary — books

- **The 4-Hour Workweek: Escape 9-5, Live Anywhere, and Join the New Rich.** Crown Publishing Group, 2007 (ISBN 9780307353139). *Expanded and Updated* edition, 2009 (ISBN 9780307465351). The foundational text: DEAL, the New Rich, lifestyle design, dreamlining, 80/20 and Parkinson's Law, elimination-before-automation, muses, mini-retirements, geoarbitrage, the low-information diet, comfort challenges, and the original fear-setting exercise.
- **The 4-Hour Body: An Uncommon Guide to Rapid Fat-Loss, Incredible Sex, and Becoming Superhuman.** Crown Archetype, 2010. Debuted #1 on the *New York Times* bestseller list. Source of the minimum effective dose, the Slow-Carb Diet, Occam's Protocol, "From Geek to Freak," and the n=1 / "human guinea pig" self-experimentation and measurement method.
- **The 4-Hour Chef: The Simple Path to Cooking Like a Pro, Learning Anything, and Living the Good Life.** New Harvest / Amazon Publishing, 2012. Source of the meta-learning frameworks DiSSS and CaFE, material-beats-method, and teacher selection.
- **Tools of Titans: The Tactics, Routines, and Habits of Billionaires, Icons, and World-Class Performers.** Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, 2016. Distillation of 100+ podcast guests; carries the "world is changed by your example" epigraph (attributed to Coelho) and reproduces Kevin Kelly's "1,000 True Fans" essay.
- **Tribe of Mentors: Short Life Advice from the Best in the World.** Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, 2017. Compiles answers from 130+ mentors to a fixed question set.
- **The Tao of Seneca: Practical Letters from a Stoic Master** (ed. Tim Ferriss), 2016. A three-volume collection of Seneca's letters; the Stoic root Ferriss cites for fear-setting.

## Primary — talk

- **"Why you should define your fears instead of your goals."** TED2017, Vancouver (conference opened April 24, 2017; posted to TED.com ~June 2017). Ferriss's most compact statement of fear-setting, the Define/Prevent/Repair structure, the cost-of-inaction analysis, and the Seneca line "we suffer more often in imagination than in reality."

## Primary — essays (tim.blog)

- **"Fear-Setting: The Most Valuable Exercise I Do Every Month."** tim.blog, May 15, 2017. The written companion to the TED talk; ties the exercise back to *The 4-Hour Workweek* and Seneca.
- **"17 Questions That Changed My Life."** tim.blog, December 2016. Source of the reframes "What would this look like if it were easy?" and "What if I did the opposite?"
- **"The 4-Hour Workweek Tools"** and the **Meta Sampler** for *The 4-Hour Chef*, tim.blog. Ferriss's own summaries confirming DEAL and the meta-learning method.

## Primary — podcast

- **The Tim Ferriss Show.** Launched April 22, 2014 (Ep. 1, Kevin Rose; Ep. 2, Josh Waitzkin). Long-form interviews deconstructing world-class performers to extract tools, tactics, and routines. Scale: ~70M downloads by 2016; crossed 900M by 2022; ~1B by 2024. Source of the recurring rapid-fire deconstruction questions.
